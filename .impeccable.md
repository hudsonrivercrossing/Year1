## Design Context
### Users
DJ (male) and YW (female); a personal first-anniversary gift celebrating their relationship. The user explicitly requested no dates, only 1 year anniversary. Visitors build the pair and watch them inhabit a small world together.
### Brand Personality
Intimate, handmade, gently sweet. Light, quiet, tactile; no product branding, page header, or promotional footer.
### Aesthetic Direction
Warm paper, muted brick red, soft olive workbench, Chinese LXGW WenKai paired with semi-connected Sacramento handwriting for English headings and the anniversary dedication, with Sacramento also used for DJ / YW names and Baskervville for English controls. Semantic rem tokens unify 16px body/action text, 14px secondary text, 12px captions and language-specific display sizes. Floating language/music controls and understated action buttons. Anniversary copy is authored for the gift, not claimed as text from the original photo, now provided as assets/reference/anniversary-frame.png. The original inscription is Experience the world together.
### Design Principles
- Let the two figures and the act of assembling tell the story.
- Keep names DJ / YW; use 1 year anniversary without a date.
- Always highlight the matching target while holding a piece; permanent silhouettes stay visible. The hints on/off control was removed by request.
- Scatter parts across the whole table; allow 360 degree orbit, panning and zooming in both assembly modes; freeze camera inertia while carrying a piece.
- Fully switch between Chinese and English without resetting play or music.
- Little Days: original cozy Japanese slice-of-life game music, a gentle cooking-game bounce, soft felt piano, restrained koto and woody percussion requested in the brief. Background music stays very quiet (0.14), off until clicked, with gentle fades and room for independent interaction cues.
- Quiet original interaction sounds are independent from music, enabled after a user gesture, with their own mute control. Floor impacts follow physics; the hug cue waits until the pair embraces.
## Mobile priority

On phones the workbench is the main subject: compact DJ/YW signature, no visible large title or subtitle, minimal one-row controls. Keep the dedication accessible. Build helpers live behind a small disclosure; play actions live in the lightweight moments menu. Default camera follows both people until the user manually orbits, pans or zooms; the frame button explicitly restores following. Preserve the desktop composition.

Mobile action choices use three compact tabs (Affection, Together, Stories), with an explicit close button and an independent automatic-interactions switch. Keep the primary hug, moments disclosure and reframe on the bottom row. Planned advanced scenes are limited to overtime, planting one flower, and DJ gifting a Hermès bag to YW; these are documented for the next round, not yet implemented.
