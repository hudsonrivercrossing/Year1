# Little life scenes

All standard meshes keep the project's .035 scene-units/LDU scale. They are
flattened from the retained official LDraw archive using `prepare_parts.py`;
original headers, dependency sources and CC BY information remain under
`public/ldraw-source/`. No source mesh is stretched to animate growth.

| Use | LDraw part |
| --- | --- |
| Desk top | 3032, plate 4 × 6 |
| Supports and chair pedestal | 3005, brick 1 × 1 |
| Single chair | 4079, minifigure seat 2 × 2 |
| Laptop base/lid and flower base | 3068b, tile 2 × 2 |
| Handbag | 93091, Friends handbag with zipper |
| Hollow gift box and removable lid | 3021 (2 × 3 plate), 3010 (1 × 4 brick), 3005 (1 × 1 brick), 3069b (1 × 2 tile) |
| Flower stem | 99249, flower stem with bar |
| Flower petals / carried seedling token | 24866, plate 1 × 1 with 5 petals |

Laptop screen/keys and the small floating heart are original geometry. The
handbag uses the unscaled official 93091 mesh, authored for LDraw by Takeshi
Takahashi (RainbowDolphin), CC BY 4.0; source headers and dependencies are retained.
Warm brown is a virtual colour, not a verified production colour. The Hermès gift
is the personal story in the bilingual text, not an official LEGO collaboration
or a claim that this part reproduces a Hermès product. No brand logo was copied.
The orange box is a project-authored assembly of standard parts, not a single
numbered LEGO gift-box part. Its lid uses a virtual lift/rotation pivot, not a
physical LEGO hinge connection.
The garden keeps up to five world-owned flowers, each with a distinct color and rigid assembly of standard 24866 flower plates on a 99249 stem. At capacity, replay dances beside an existing flower without immediately repeating the previous choice. Rebuild clears the garden.
Furniture, laptop and bag belong to one transient scene and are removed on
normal completion, cancellation and rebuild. Gift transfer moves one world-space
instance continuously before changing its owner; original held accessories use
the existing shared drop/recovery transaction.

## Illustrated catalog

The public [README parts gallery](../../README.md#小小生活里的道具) includes
renderings of the current assembled props and selected standard base parts.
The bag and box thumbnails render the actual game geometry. The box has a real
hollow cavity with 1.4 × 1.68 × .7 scene-unit clearance; the full-scale bag is
1.333 × 1.138 × .681. It rises vertically through the open lid before turning
into the hand and the box is removed before presentation. The complete box footprint and the unwrapping stance fit
inside the existing
3.4-unit interaction clearing, so navigation and clearing algorithms stay intact.
The full box is still registered as a conservative circular obstacle while visible.
